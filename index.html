Hang Yu 余航

Ph.D. Candidate · Tufts University · HRI & Robot Learning

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Tufts University, advised by Dr. Elaine Short in the Assistive Agent and Behavior Learning lab. I study how robots can learn from ordinary people: their feedback, their demonstrations, and, increasingly, the outputs of vision-language models. Most of my datasets are open-sourced and were collected in public spaces with real-world, non-expert users. Right now I am a research intern at ABB Robotics, working on VLA models for bimanual manipulation.

Volume 01About

About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Tufts University, advised by Dr. Elaine Short in the Assistive Agent and Behavior Learning lab (AABL). I work on improving robot learning from multi-modular human teaching, including human feedback, human demonstrations, and outputs from VLM/LLM. Most of my data is open-sourced and collected from public spaces with real-world non-expert users.

Actively open to research collaborations in HRI, RLHF for robots, and human data collection methodology.
